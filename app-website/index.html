KIMEP Mobile

An unofficial Android client for KIMEP students.

Schedule, grades, academic calendar and class reminders — without using the old student portal.

Download APK GitHub

Unofficial project — not affiliated with or endorsed by KIMEP University.

Features

  • ScheduleWeekly timetable grouped by day, with today highlighted and a midterm-week notice. Opens from cache and updates in the background.
  • Grades & goalsGPA, progress toward 146 graduation credits, current scores and your transcript. Pick a grade goal to see the required average, with linked remaining-score sliders.
  • Privacy & updatesNo usage tracking in v1.0.1. Read the privacy notice and check for updates whenever you want, from Settings.
  • Academic calendarThe official calendar parsed into the app, for both academic years and every semester.
  • Final examsA dedicated view for the exam timetable, once the university publishes it.
  • Class remindersNotifications one hour and ten minutes before each class, plus a final-exam reminder. Each can be switched off.
  • Material YouNative Material 3 design that follows your phone's colours, light and dark.

Inside the app

Sample data The v1.0.1 interface, captured on a real phone with a fictional student enrolled in six courses (18 credits). Course metadata comes from the F2026 catalog. The demo build is not distributed.

Privacy

v1.0.1 removes all usage tracking. The privacy notice lives in Settings and explains connections to KIMEP and GitHub and data stored on your device. Your account connects directly to KIMEP over HTTPS; the app does not save your password.

v1.0.0 and earlier releases may still send anonymous usage statistics. On those builds, turn off anonymous statistics in Settings → Privacy. Source changes do not change installed APKs; upgrade to v1.0.1 for the tracking-free app.
